Our standard

A beautiful room begins long before the first coat.

The quality of a painted surface depends on what happens underneath it. That is why we place equal attention on inspection, preparation and application.

Cracks, damaged areas, loose coatings and uneven surfaces are identified before work begins. The correct preparation and paint system are then selected for the room, surface and conditions.

Attention to detail

Clean lines. Even coverage. A composed result.

Premium painting is not defined by the paint alone. It is visible in the edges, transitions, surface consistency and the way the work relates to the architecture of the room.

Floors, furniture and fixed elements are protected before painting begins. Work areas are kept organised throughout the project and the property is reviewed before completion.

Colour guidance

The right white is not always white.

Natural light, floor tones, furniture and neighbouring colours can completely change how a finish appears inside the room.

We help narrow the direction before work begins, reducing uncertain choices and creating a result that feels connected to the property.

How it works

A controlled process
from survey to handover.

You know what is included, how the project will progress and what result the agreed scope is designed to deliver.

  1. 01

    Survey

    We inspect the property, measure the work and assess the condition of the surfaces.

  2. 02

    Specification

    The preparation, paint system, colours and required finish are agreed.

  3. 03

    Preparation

    Work areas are protected and surfaces are repaired, prepared and primed where required.

  4. 04

    Application

    Paint is applied systematically with attention to coverage, consistency and clean detailing.

  5. 05

    Handover

    The completed work is reviewed and the property is left organised for its next use.
