Furnished homes

The logistics are considered before the painting begins.

A furnished property requires a different plan from an empty one.

We review furniture volume, access, protection and the sequence of rooms during the survey. Smaller items may need to be removed by the client before work starts.

Where a property contains substantial or heavy furniture, a separate moving team may be required. This will be identified and agreed before the project is confirmed.

The initial survey

A useful first conversation—not a sales visit.

The purpose of the survey is to understand the property, identify the real scope and determine whether TRUECOAT is the right fit for the project.

  1. 01

    Project review

    We review your location, property type, photographs and requested service.

  2. 02

    Initial contact

    We contact you to clarify the scope and confirm whether an on-site or remote survey is appropriate.

  3. 03

    Property assessment

    Surfaces, access, room conditions and wider interior requirements are assessed.

  4. 04

    Tailored proposal

    You receive a proposal based on the agreed work, finishes and project requirements.
